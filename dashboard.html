Resume Tips

Pro Tips to Get Noticed

🎯
Use keywords from the job description. ATS filters look for specific terms — mirror the language of the posting.
📏
Keep it to one page unless you have 10+ years of experience. Recruiters spend under 10 seconds on a first pass.
📊
Quantify your achievements. "Increased sales by 40%" beats "responsible for sales" every time.
🔗
Always include LinkedIn and GitHub. Recruiters check these — make sure they're polished too.
